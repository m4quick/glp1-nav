Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

GLP-1 Navigator is published by M4Quick LLC. This policy explains what information is collected when you use this site, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information you give us

The only way to send us personal information directly is by emailing us. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and we use it solely to reply and act on your message. We do not add you to mailing lists and we do not sell or share it. Please do not send us sensitive personal health information — we do not need it, and email is not a secure medium for it.

2. The protein calculator

Our protein calculator runs entirely in your browser. The weight, height, activity level and medication details you enter are used to perform the calculation on your own device and are never transmitted to us, never stored, and never logged. Closing the page discards them.

This site displays advertising served by Google, and Google's advertising products use cookies and similar technologies. Specifically:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • More detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services is available in Google's partner-sites policy.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may behave differently as a result.

4. Analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand aggregate site usage — which pages are read, and roughly where visitors arrive from. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally, and it never includes anything you type into the calculator.

Some product links on this site are Amazon affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. When you follow such a link, Amazon may set its own cookies and will handle your activity on its site under its own privacy notice, not this one. A commission never influences which products or medications we describe — see our editorial policy.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information a site holds about you. Because we do not maintain user accounts and the calculator stores nothing, in most cases we hold no personal information about you beyond any email correspondence. If you have emailed us and would like that correspondence deleted, contact us and we will do so.

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
