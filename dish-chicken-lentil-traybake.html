Chicken and lentil traybake

The highest-protein thing on this site for the least attention. Everything goes in one tin and the oven does the rest.

Shortcuts for the days cooking is not going to happen. No pan, no washing up.

How

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Toss the vegetables with the oil in a roasting tin.
  2. Sit the chicken on top, season, and give it 20 minutes.
  3. Stir the lentils in around the chicken and return it for 5 minutes to warm through.
  4. Check the chicken is cooked through before serving.

💡 Worth knowing

Cook two and keep one cold. A cooked chicken breast in the fridge is the difference between hitting your protein and giving up on it.
