Smoked salmon over cold lentils

The most protein here for the least effort, and the lentils bring the fibre that most cold meals do not.

How

  1. Tip the lentils onto a plate straight from the pouch.
  2. Drape the salmon over and tuck the cucumber in around it.
  3. Splash the oil over and add a squeeze of lemon.

💡 Worth knowing

Smoked salmon is salty, which is often welcome when nothing tastes of much -- but it is the saltiest thing on this site, so it is not an everyday meal.
