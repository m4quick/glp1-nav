Chickpeas and boiled eggs with cucumber

Boil the eggs whenever you feel well and keep them in the fridge. Then this is assembly, not cooking.

  • Chickpeashalf a tin, rinsed 8 g
  • Boiled eggs2 eggs, hard-boiled and cooled 13 g
  • Cucumbera short length, sliced —

How

  1. Rinse the chickpeas and tip them onto a plate.
  2. Halve the eggs and set them on top with the cucumber.
  3. Splash the oil over, and plenty of black pepper.

💡 Worth knowing

Boil six eggs at once on a good day. Cold hard-boiled eggs keep about a week and turn several bad days into a two-minute meal.
