Cold prawns with edamame and avocado

Bought ready-cooked, assembled in a bowl. No heat, no smell, and a lot of protein for something that takes four minutes.

  • Cooked prawnsa small pack, cooked and peeled 25 g

How

  1. Run the edamame under the cold tap to thaw them, or leave them out for ten minutes.
  2. Tip everything into a bowl and slice the avocado over the top.

💡 Worth knowing

Frozen edamame thaw in a sieve under cold water in about a minute, which is the only reason this is a four-minute meal rather than a twenty-minute one.
