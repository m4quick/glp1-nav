Cold protein bowl, no cooking

For the mornings when the thought of cooking is worse than the thought of eating. Nothing here needs heat, and it takes about two minutes.

27 gprotein
340calories
2 minhands on
1serves
  • Greek Yogurt200 g, plain 20 g
  • Frozen berriesa small handful 1 g
  • Almondsabout 20 g 4 g
  • Chia seeds1 tbsp 2 g

How

  1. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl.
  2. Scatter the berries over — straight from frozen is fine, they thaw as you eat.
  3. Add the almonds and chia and leave it a minute if you like it thicker.

Shortcuts for the days cooking is not going to happen. Similar protein, no pan.
